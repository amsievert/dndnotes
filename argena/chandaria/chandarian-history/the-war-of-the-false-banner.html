The War of the False Banner

The War of the False Banner, so named by the Coalition of Nations, started in the fall of 2519 PS, with the Larenthian invasion of the Kingdom of Rodigia. The war continued through the winter and would eventually end in the early spring of 2520 PS, with the Battle of the Daggers.

Combatants

The two core combatants of the war were the Larenthian Alliance and the Kingdom of Rodigia. The Larenthian Alliance fought mostly alone, but did use its deep pockets to reinforce its ranks with mercenaries from Norlund. On the other side of the war, the Kingdom of Rodigia allied with the Kingdom of Falstor, Valdens, and the Republic of No'zaral. These nations would form the Coalition of Nations.

Casus Belli

In the Holfirth province, merchant caravans started being attacked regularly in the summer of 2519. On the 26th of Florum, goblins following the hobgoblin warlord Kardag attacked the Arena Monstera in Holfirth with explosives, killing many. At the time, this attack was attributed to a prominent Rodigian merchant named Larold.

Using the fear generated from this attack, the Larenthian government sent an army to Rodigia to besiege the Rodigian capital city of Rhugnurt. It would later be revealed that Ileus had been behind both the attack on the Arena Monstera, and the framing of Larold. He had fabricated the Rodigian aggression to justify sending an army to Rodigia as a cover to search for the Hammer of Epius.

Timeline

War was officially declared on the 7th of Otun, 2519, when the Larenthian Army crossed the Southern Strait into Rodigian lands. Most of the fighting that happened in the war was small skirmishes, with only a few large battles taking place.

The Siege of Rhugnurt

On the 11th of Otun, 2519, the Larenthian army set up a siege camp at the base of Mount Thogrin. The army consisted of 45,000 Larenthian soldiers under the command of General Blaith Smil. They would begin assaulting the city walls.

Rhugnurt held strong for months. Mount Thogrin has one road up to the city, and the giants built strong defenses, rolling boulders and logs down the mountain path to repel waves of attackers. As the giants fell back to the city proper, the massive stone walls would hold the besieging army for the rest of the fight.

On the 21st of Planimor, 2519, rising tension between the army's leadership came to a head and the army split. Nearly 20,000 Larenthian soldiers had died, and the city was no closer to being taken. A contingency of commanders withdrew their soldiers and retreated back to Larenthia, leaving General Smil behind with about 12,000 soldiers. Smil would retreat the rest of his forces 5 days later, officially ending the siege.

The Winter Rebuild

After regrouping near Crone's Cross in Larenthia, the wounded army settled in for the winter. During the next few months, Larenthian military doctrine would change dramatically. The nation's head diviner, Dormar Talovel, would begin a new initiative to train Larenthian soldiers in basic arcane spellcasting. The city of Wizendell was turned into a wartime factory producing textbooks and instructors to be sent to the army academies across the nation, and also to the army already stationed at Crone's Cross.

The Forming of the Coalition of Nations

In early 2520, the nations of Rodigia, Falstor, Valdens, and No'zaral met in Spellhaven to discuss what to do about the Larenthian aggression. After much deliberation, they decided to form the Coalition of Nations to fight back against the Larenthian Alliance. The newly formed Coalition began planning their first counterattack.

The Spring Offensive

While the Coalition's leadership were focused on planning an invasion of Larenthia, the Alliance were already mobilizing against them. Splitting their forces in two, one army marched towards the Kha'anj Oasis, and the other force sailed towards Dalonstor.

Splitting the Larenthian armies turned out to be a costly blunder, as the mercenary army sent towards the Kha'anj was quickly destroyed by the dug-in defenders. The raid on Dalonstor would prove to be slightly more successful. The Alliance was able to leverage their powerful navy to overwhelm the Falstor defenses, and bombarded the city for a whole night. It would take reinforcements from Rhugnurt to drive the Larenthian navy out of the Dalonstor bay, ending the attack.

Counterattack

On the 19th of Hemlia, Coalition forces invaded Wizendell from the sea and through teleportation. A spy was able to establish a teleportation anchor within the Divinaire Campus, allowing a special task force of Coalition forces to teleport in and attack from the inside. The attack was an overwhelming success, and Wizendell was completely occupied by the Coalition army.

In the following days, a Larenthian envoy arrived to announce a formal declaration of war. Now that the Coalition was a clearly defined group, the Larenthians felt a declaration of war was necessary.

The Battle of the Plains

With an occupying force close to their capital city, the Larenthian army packed up and marched south from Crone's Cross, hoping to invade the Coalition nations by land. Coalition scouts alerted the army of this movement, and a Coalition force amassed in the Kha'anj to meet the Larenthian army in the field.

On the 14th of Verna, 2520, the armies of both sides clashed in the plains just south of the Fallen Daggers. The Coalition army was able to draw Ileus's simulacra into the fighting, allowing their special task force to engage the real Ileus in his demiplane. The fighting on the material plane continued, with both sides taking heavy casualties. Eventually, Ileus was killed, and his simulacra were destroyed. Without these powerful archmage simulacra, the Larenthian army was routed, retreating back towards the mountains.

Conclusion

The war ended soon after the Battle of the Plains, when Chief Magnate Thedric Alfir of the Larenthian Alliance signed the treaty of Rhugnurt, officially ending the war. Alfir would abdicate the throne, and no economic sanctions would be levied against the Alliance.

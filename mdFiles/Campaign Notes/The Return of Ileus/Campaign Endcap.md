## The Battle in the Scorch
The climactic battle of the [[The War of the False Banner|War of the False Banner]] took place in the eastern [[The Eternal Scorch|Scorch]], just south of the Fallen Daggers mountain range. Forces from the Coalition met forces from the Larenthian Alliance on the battlefield, drawing Ileus' simulacra into combat. Using this opportunity, Astarius, Saraq, Utu, Holfog, and William Clegane Jr infiltrated Spellreach, Ileus's demiplanar lair. Following a very lengthy fight, Ileus was defeated. Saraq was also killed but revived by Clegane Jr as Spellreach was being destroyed. As a last act of defiance, Ileus broke the Staff of Deception over his knee and was teleported along with the party to the side of the [[Exalted Summit]], where he looked upon the ruins of his home one last time before being struck down by the combined power of the [[Gods and Mythos (Aeonic Pantheon)|Aeonic Gods]], his body and soul completely destroyed. Following this, an argument broke out among the party about how to allocate the remaining Relics of Dissension. Utu attempted to take three Relics with him to reclaim his soul from the Blood Chief, but the party tried to stop him. Soon after, mages from the Coalition arrived and helped the party subdue Utu.

## Postwar Chandaria
The remaining Relics of Dissension were scattered across the planes by the party members. The rest of the continent was left to rebuild broken relationships and destroyed cities. 

With King Offal having been gravely wounded in the battle in the [[The Eternal Scorch|Scorch]], [[Falstor (Kingdom)|Falstor]] found itself weakened. The Alystri, while still mourning the death of Stefan Alystri, provided essential support to the Kingdom, gaining great favor and cementing themselves as a great power in house politics.

After the return of an Ancient Archmage, Spellhaven deliberated for a long time on its policies regarding the [[The Anteitic Principles of Magic|Anteitic Principles of Magic]]. They also drafted an emergency plan should another mage ever become a world-ending threat. This plan was drafted and put into action by Hal Venner the evoker, having fought on the front lines of the [[The War of the False Banner|War of the False Banner]].

Larenthia would seek to redefine itself following the war. Chief Magnate Thedric Alfir willingly abdicated the throne, and an interim council of Merchant Lords formed to maintain order while a new leader was chosen.

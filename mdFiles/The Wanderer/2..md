The forest grew thick fast. Solhurst Forest was often called World's Edge because it was believed by many in the East that nothing existed beyond it. Gray scoffed at this name. He had visited many towns in the Western Reaches on his past explorations looking for the First Builders. While the towns and villages were independent of the nations of the East, they often traded with each other, forming trade agreements between themselves. Gray frequently wondered how he would fair settling down in one of these towns. Parts of it sounded nice, but he knew deep down that his curiosity could never allow him to stay in one place. Ultimately, he enjoyed traveling the world too much. He had had enough of civilization at the university in Spellhaven. While invaluable to his understanding of the natural world, those days were past.
